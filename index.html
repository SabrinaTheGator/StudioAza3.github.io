03 — Read Me

Terms of
Service

Payment is acknowledgment of these terms. If anything is unclear, ask before you commission.

All comissions are for personal use only unless a commercial license is purchased, I reserve the right to decline a commission if I see it fit, Slots can be transferable only before payment, The illustrations i make will not be posted publicly unless I have permission of the commissioner

Prices are listed in USD and will be paid via Paypal, The prices can be negotiable if it doesnt sound too unreasonable. Payments are made in two instances, the first half of the pay is done after we confirm the looks of the sketch, the second half of the pay is after i give you a preview of the work, once the full payment is done i will send a google drive file with the art itself, the save file, the timelapse, and the alts if the commission includes them

Once a sketch is delivered, no refunds will be issued. If I am unable to complete your commission due to personal circumstances, a full refund will be provided.

You may use the artwork for personal use, profile pictures, and non-commercial sharing with credit. Commercial use (merchandise, games, books) requires a commercial license discussed at time of commission. Do not remove my signature or claim the work as your own.

Will draw: Illustrations, pinups, portraits, sfw/nsfw, OC's, sketches, linearts, Simple backgrounds (swirls, paint splatter, zoomed out character etc) and full colors/renders.

Won't draw: Loli/Shota (in any context), Real people in NSFW scenarios, Hate symbols, Revenge Porn, Logos, Thing outside of my artstyle (like for example Photorealism, Enviroments, Etc), Heavy fetishes like Scat, Inflation, Vore, or something that would make me uncomfortable, Exaggerated sized genitalia, Non consensual, AI generated references for commissions, anything else feel free to ask
